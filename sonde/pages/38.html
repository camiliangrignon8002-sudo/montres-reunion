French real estate: apartments, villas, houses

As the top tourist destination worldwide, real estate in France offers its visitors a rich historic and cultural heritage. From Marseille to Brest, from Biarritz to Metz, or from Toulouse to Lille, French real estate presents a diverse array of regions, each with its unique characteristics and architecture. Townhouses, country houses, old mills, coastal villas, city center apartments, stately homes, manors, or castles: you will find a wide variety of French homes for sale in France, and Green-Acres.com is the ideal site for your property purchase!

Provence Alpes Côte d'Azur property for sale

The PACA region is a mecca for international tourism, and you'll find all kinds of properties here. If you have the budget, you may be tempted by a beautiful coastal villa on the heights of Marseille and Saint Tropez. If not, let yourself be charmed by the houses for sale in Provence,in France.

The Côte d'Azur continues to be a popular destination in the property market, attracting buyers from all over the world in search of luxury real estate, a Mediterranean lifestyle and a great investment opportunity. With its picturesque landscapes, stretches of golden sand and generous amount of sunshine, the area offers a verdant environment.

The region offers a variety of leisure activities that enable visitors and residents alike to make the most of all it has to offer, whether on land or sea. These include water sports, with its crystal-clear waters and spectacular coastline; hiking and cycling, with its trails, hills and hilltop villages; and cultural visits, including museums, art galleries, historic sites and festivals. Finally, the nightlife offers trendy bars, elegant nightclubs and glamorous casinos, providing after-dark entertainment.

Property for sale Languedoc

Sea, mountains, blue skies, varied landscapes, abundant vineyards and good road network are just some of the many assets of this region. Real estate in Languedoc Roussillon is a good investment because between holidaymakers and students there is always a demand for rentals. Languedoc-Roussillon, located in the south of France, is a region rich in history and cultural diversity. From Montpellier to Perpignan, via the medieval city of Carcassonne, each city offers a unique charm and varied opportunities.

Close to the Spanish border at Le Grau-du-Roi, this region benefits from a strategic location and a sunny climate. Languedoc-Roussillon is an ideal place to make a real estate project a reality. The real estate market in Languedoc-Roussillon is dynamic, attracting many buyers looking for primary or secondary residences. Buying here means choosing an exceptional quality of life in the heart of magnificent landscapes.

Property for sale in Aquitaine France

A region of contrasts between the sea and the mountains, Aquitaine is a rich land with varied landscapes. Property in Aquitaine is also very varied. All types of houses for sale are available from the Landes or Basque style house to the Béarn or Périgord style farmhouse. After being an English territory for some time, the Duchy of Aquitaine was definitively attached to the French crown. It is a real paradise for property where it is good to live all year round, or simply for the holidays. Surfers, skiers, hikers, gourmets and lovers of old monuments find what they want here.

Property for sale French Alps

Nature reserves, lakes and unique sites like Mont Blanc or the Ardeche Gorges are just some of the many assets that this privileged region benefits from. It is a paradise for experienced climbers and skiers. It is also a special destination for lovers of fine food and top class wines. And let us not forget Lyon, ancient capital of Roman Gaul and remarkable capital with a rich architectural heritage. If property in some areas of Rhone-Alpes remains expensive, it represents nevertheless an excellent investment because the chalets in Méribel, or the apartments in Megève can be rented in summer as well as in winter. Houses for sale in Lyon, on the heights of Fourvières are also much sought after, but in other areas of Rhone-Alpes, it is possible to find farms or houses for sale at very affordable prices.

Property for sale Midi-Pyrénées France

For the inhabitants of Midi-Pyrenees, the splendid landscapes of the region are a normal feature. The same rule applies to the gastronomy, where cassoulet, foie gras, truffles, armagnac or monbazillac seem to be part of daily life. A house for sale in Toulouse? Cultural dynamism and effervescence will be on the program. Otherwise: quiet and authenticity. Each corner of this vast and rich region has its own traditions and its own architecture. This is why property in Midi-Pyrenees is very varied and the choice of houses for sale varies according to place: old shepherds' houses, the rectangular houses of Quercy or 'Petites Toulousaines' (small Toulouse houses), to mention just a few.

Ile-de-France Property

Ile-de-France is an amazing destination and home to the iconic city of Paris, offering a variety of real estate options. From luxury apartments in the city centre to beautiful country homes on the outskirts, there is something for everyone. Whether you’re looking for a stylish flat near the Champs Elysées or a charming cottage amongst rolling hills and vineyards, Ile-de-France can offer it all. With excellent transportation connections and close proximity to major attractions such as The Eiffel Tower and Versailles Palace, Ile-de-France is perfect for those seeking luxury living with easy access to exciting urban locations. Enjoy all that this stunning region has to offer by investing in property in Ile de France today!

Poitou-Charentes Property

Poitou-Charentes in France is an ideal place to live while experiencing a unique charm and culture. Located on the Western coast of France, it offers stunning landscapes of rolling hills and rivers, as well as beautiful sandy beaches along the French Atlantic coastline. Whether you’re looking for a holiday home or permanent residence, Poitou-Charentes offers something for everyone with its mild climate, bustling cities and quiet villages. Experience all that this charming region has to offer - from traditional markets, local cuisine and wine tasting activities to day trips exploring chateaus and forests. For those seeking real estate opportunities in Europe – look no further than Poitou-Charentes!
